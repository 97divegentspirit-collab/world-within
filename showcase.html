Bite By Bytes Economy — Start small, Grow Big

Pioneered by Mr. Mazien E. Alabi

It began at a compound gate in Lagos, watching a boy work hard every single day — sweeping, carrying, running errands — and still have nothing to show for it. Not from laziness. From living inside a system that was never built to catch small effort and turn it into something visible. Bite by Bytes was built to be that system.

BBB offers Bite by Bytes began from a simple vision: A growth ecosystem, not a gig app. Young people complete real micro-tasks — voice recordings, image labeling, caption writing, local research — and every contribution builds a visible reputation, a skill record, and real earnings over time. Minors are protected by a transparent public agreement covering hours, pay, and privacy, published openly before anyone joins. Small effort, every day, until one day it becomes visible.
